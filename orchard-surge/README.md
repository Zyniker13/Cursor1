# Orchard Surge

A short run-based action game. Between nights you plant. Plants are both the guns and the lanterns. Unlit tiles hide pests; the pests are still there. Shroud moths stay hidden even in ordinary light. Rind moths stay hidden until a briar cracks them. Pitch moths stay sealed until a flare melts them. Frost moths stay sealed until a hearth thaws them. Web moths stay sealed until a shear cuts them. Glass moths stay sealed until a chime cracks them. Scale moths stay sealed until a rasp strips them. Mail moths stay sealed until a maul cracks them. Hide moths stay sealed until a bill splits them. Dusk moths stay sealed until a shade covers them. The shade is not a light. Veil moths stay sealed until a cowl covers them. The cowl is not a light. Pall moths stay sealed until a hood covers them. The hood is not a light. Gloom moths stay sealed until a cloak covers them. The cloak is not a light. Murk moths stay sealed until a cape covers them. The cape is not a light. Dread moths stay sealed until a shawl covers them. The shawl is not a light. Woe moths stay sealed until a stole covers them. The stole is not a light. Blight moths stay sealed until a mantle covers them. The mantle is not a light. Rot moths stay sealed until a tippet covers them. The tippet is not a light. Mildew moths stay sealed until a wimple covers them. The wimple is not a light. Rust moths stay sealed until an alb covers them. The alb is not a light. Scab moths stay sealed until an amice covers them. The amice is not a light. Spot moths stay sealed until an orphrey covers them. The orphrey is not a light. Scald moths stay sealed until a maniple covers them. The maniple is not a light. Blister moths stay sealed until a chasuble covers them. The chasuble is not a light.

This is a vertical slice: one gardener, twenty-eight plants, fifty-one nights, fog. A run is a few minutes. There is no meta unlock tree.

## Simulation

`sim/orchard_sim.gd` is a fixed 30 Hz simulation with no scene tree. Combat, light, planting, and damage all step there. `scenes/main.gd` only samples input, calls `step`, and draws. The same seed and the same commands reproduce the same run. Spawn edges are the only random draws, and they come from a seeded generator.

A tile is lit when its rectangle meets any plant’s light radius. That updates every tick. A pest on an unlit tile is flagged hidden and is not drawn. Hidden pests still move and can deal contact damage. Plants shoot the nearest visible pest inside their weapon range.

A shroud moth is the same pest with one extra rule. Ordinary light, including the lantern, does not reveal it and shots do not hurt it, splash included. A wick burns the shroud only while the moth’s tile meets that wick’s light. Outside that light the shroud closes again. Contact damage still lands, so a shroud you never burn will reach you and the night cannot end.

A rind moth is the same pest with a different lock. Ordinary light does not reveal it, and neither does a wick. Shots do not hurt it, splash included, until a briar cracks the tile it stands on. Outside that light the rind closes again. A moth that is both shroud and rind stays closed until the tile is in wick light and briar light at once. Either light alone leaves it shut. Contact damage still lands, so a rind you never crack will reach you and the night cannot end.

A pitch moth is the same pest with a third lock. Ordinary light does not reveal it, and neither does a wick or a briar. Shots do not hurt it, splash included, until a flare melts the tile it stands on. Outside that light the pitch seals again. A moth that carries more than one lock stays closed until every lock is open on that tile. Contact damage still lands, so a pitch you never melt will reach you and the night cannot end.
