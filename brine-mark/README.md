# Brine Mark

Indirect colony sim, Salt Coast slice. One coast, a wet season, a short dry season, a cart beat, a ship beat, a market beat, a yard beat, a bunk beat, a loft beat, a gate beat, a wage beat, a relief beat, a watch beat, a lamp beat, a bell beat, a horn beat, a flag beat, a banner beat, a pennant beat, a standard beat, a jack beat, an ensign beat, a burgee beat, a cornet beat, a guidon beat, a fanion beat, a pennon beat, one quota of covered salt. You paint the flats. Ants follow the mark under their feet. You do not select them.

## Play

Requires Godot 4 (`godot` on `PATH`). This project was run with Godot 4.7.

From this directory:

```bash
godot --path .
```

Rake the shore beside the brine during the wet season. Ants pile loose salt there. Stack moves loose salt into stacks. Cover keeps stacked salt when the rain arrives; loose salt is destroyed. The first rain banks whatever was covered and pulls the brine back. Shore rakes go idle. Pan marks crystallize loose salt on the drying flats until the second rain, and the same stack and cover marks move that salt under cover. Trail is how ants cross from the anthill to those marks. The countdown above the flats names the season and refills when the dry season starts. After the second rain, a cart beat opens and the countdown refills. Paint a crib. Workers carry the banked salt — the first-rain haul and whatever the dry season still holds under cover — into that crib. A crib is shut until the cart beat, so it does not change the earlier haul. A coast with no crib leaves the bank where it is. After the cart beat, a ship beat opens and the countdown refills. Paint a ship. Workers load the salt sitting in the crib onto that ship. A ship is shut until the ship beat, so it does not change the wet haul, the dry haul, or the cart. A crib with no ship keeps the salt. After the ship beat, a market beat opens and the countdown refills. Paint a stall. Workers sell the salt already on the ship, one unit at a time, at one coin per unit. A stall is shut until the market beat, so it does not change the wet haul, the dry haul, the cart, or the shipment. A ship with no stall keeps the salt, and the coin total stays 0. After the market beat, a yard beat opens and the countdown refills. Paint a yard. Workers bank the coins already earned, one coin at a time. A yard is shut until the yard beat, so it does not change the wet haul, the dry haul, the cart, the shipment, or the earned coins. A purse with no yard stays at the stall, and the yard total stays 0. After the yard beat, a bunk beat opens and the countdown refills. Paint a bunk one cell north of the yard. Workers hire from the coins already banked, in a fixed order, at 100 coins for one hire. A bunk is shut until the bunk beat, so it does not change the wet haul, the dry haul, the cart, the shipment, or the earned coins. One bunk hires every full hundred and leaves the remainder in the yard. A purse with no bunk hires nobody, and the earned coin total is still reported. The slice population cap is 40. Flats, ants, salt, the flag, the banner, the pennant, the standard, the jack, the ensign, the burgee, the cornet, the guidon, the fanion, and the pennon are drawn with Godot primitives. The banner glyph is a crossbar and a wider cloth. The pennant glyph is a short staff and a small triangular fly. The standard glyph is a tall pale staff and a small dark cloth. The jack glyph is a small dark flag on a short pale hoist. The ensign glyph is a small pale flag with a dark fly. The burgee glyph is a swallowtail: two dark tails on a pale cloth. The cornet glyph is a long pale streamer with a dark hoist. The guidon glyph is a forked pale cloth with a dark head, shorter than the cornet's streamer and distinct from the burgee's two dark tails. The fanion glyph is a small square pale cloth on a short dark staff, distinct from the guidon's fork, the cornet's long streamer, the burgee's swallowtail, and the pennant's triangle. The pennon glyph is a long narrow pale fly that tapers to one point on a short dark staff, distinct from the fanion's square, the guidon's fork, the cornet's streamer, the burgee's swallowtail, and the pennant's triangle.

## Tests

From this directory. Godot's exit code is non-zero when a check fails.

```bash
godot --headless --path . -s res://tests/run_tests.gd
```

The checks are:

- A scripted paint plan in `tests/plans.json` meets the covered-salt quota after both rains.
- An all-rake plan misses the quota, and the summary string names uncovered salt.
- A pan plan with no rakes stores nothing in the wet season and meets the quota in the dry season. It fails if pans do not crystallize, or if they crystallize while the brine is still in.
- A continued plan keeps the wet-season haul and adds at least another quota from pans.
- A plan that paints pans only after the first rain still meets the quota.
- The winning shore does not gain salt once the brine has pulled back.
- The winning shore and the dry-pan plan still store the same salt they did before the cart beat, and their summaries stay the same. Neither paints a crib.
- A plan that paints no crib fails the cart check. The bank does not move.
- The winning shore plus a crib keeps that same haul and moves the whole bank into the crib. Painting the crib when the cart beat opens moves the same amount.
- A dry-pan plan plus a crib keeps a wet haul of 0 and moves the dry-season bank off the covers into the crib.
- The winning shore, the dry-pan plan, and the cart plans still report the same wet haul, dry haul, and carted salt.
- A plan that fills the crib and paints no ship fails the ship check. The salt stays in the crib.
- The carted shore plus one ship north of the crib keeps that wet haul, dry haul, and cart, and loads the whole crib onto the ship. Painting the ship when the ship beat opens loads the same amount.
- A dry-pan plan plus a crib and a ship keeps a wet haul of 0 and loads the dry-season crib onto the ship.
- The winning shore, the dry pans, the cart plans, and the ship plans still report the same wet haul, dry haul, carted salt, and shipped salt.
- A plan that ships salt and paints no stall fails the market check. The salt stays on the ship and the coin total stays 0.
