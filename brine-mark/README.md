# Brine Mark

Indirect colony sim, Salt Coast slice. One coast, a wet season, a short dry season, a cart beat, a ship beat, a market beat, a yard beat, a bunk beat, a loft beat, a gate beat, a wage beat, a relief beat, a watch beat, a lamp beat, a bell beat, a horn beat, a flag beat, a banner beat, a pennant beat, a standard beat, a jack beat, an ensign beat, a burgee beat, a cornet beat, a guidon beat, a fanion beat, a pennon beat, a gonfalon beat, an oriflamme beat, a vexillum beat, a labarum beat, a signum beat, a streamer beat, a bannerol beat, a pendant beat, a wheft beat, one quota of covered salt. You paint the flats. Ants follow the mark under their feet. You do not select them.

## Play

From this directory:

Rake the shore beside the brine during the wet season. Ants pile loose salt there. Stack moves loose salt into stacks. Cover keeps stacked salt when the rain arrives; loose salt is destroyed. The first rain banks whatever was covered and pulls the brine back. Shore rakes go idle. Pan marks crystallize loose salt on the drying flats until the second rain, and the same stack and cover marks move that salt under cover. Trail is how ants cross from the anthill to those marks. The countdown above the flats names the season and refills when the dry season starts. After the second rain, a cart beat opens and the countdown refills. Paint a crib. Workers carry the banked salt — the first-rain haul and whatever the dry season still holds under cover — into that crib. A crib is shut until the cart beat, so it does not change the earlier haul. A coast with no crib leaves the bank where it is. After the cart beat, a ship beat opens and the countdown refills. Paint a ship. Workers load the salt sitting in the crib onto that ship. A ship is shut until the ship beat, so it does not change the wet haul, the dry haul, or the cart. A crib with no ship keeps the salt. After the ship beat, a market beat opens and the countdown refills. Paint a stall. Workers sell the salt already on the ship, one unit at a time, at one coin per unit. A stall is shut until the market beat, so it does not change the wet haul, the dry haul, the cart, or the shipment. A ship with no stall keeps the salt, and the coin total stays 0. After the market beat, a yard beat opens and the countdown refills. Paint a yard. Workers bank the coins already earned, one coin at a time. A yard is shut until the yard beat, so it does not change the wet haul, the dry haul, the cart, the shipment, or the earned coins. A purse with no yard stays at the stall, and the yard total stays 0. After the yard beat, a bunk beat opens and the countdown refills. Paint a bunk one cell north of the yard. Workers hire from the coins already banked, in a fixed order, at 100 coins for one hire. A bunk is shut until the bunk beat, so it does not change the wet haul, the dry haul, the cart, the shipment, or the earned coins. One bunk hires every full hundred and leaves the remainder in the yard. A purse with no bunk hires nobody, and the earned coin total is still reported.
