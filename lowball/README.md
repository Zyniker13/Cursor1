# Lowball

An eighteen-week double round-robin, then a best-of-three final, of an invented three-inning bat-and-ball sport. You run **Lumen Reedmice** in **Lumen Quay**, with **Southspur Glass**, **Pellwick Cinders**, **Marrowgate Herons**, **Saltmere Kites**, **Ashfen Moths**, **Reedfen Larks**, **Cinderwharf Pikes**, **Saltwharf Gulls**, and **Marrowfen Owls**. Players are generated. The clubs, the town, and the names are fictional.

Five matches are played each week for eighteen weeks, and no club has a bye. Weeks 1–9 are a round-robin, and weeks 10–18 repeat those pairings with home reversed, so every club plays every week and meets every other club once at home and once away. The office lists the whole schedule and the table from the start. Home is listed first and bats second. Batting second gives no stat bonus. You watch or skip your match. The other four matches that week are recorded with it.

A win is 2 points and a tie is 1. After week 18 the clubs are ordered by points per game, then run difference, then the series seed. Rates are compared as fractions. An equal rate is not ahead. Divide the series seed by 10 and keep the remainder: 0 starts with Lumen Reedmice, 1 with Southspur Glass, 2 with Pellwick Cinders, 3 with Marrowgate Herons, 4 with Saltmere Kites, 5 with Ashfen Moths, 6 with Reedfen Larks, 7 with Cinderwharf Pikes, 8 with Saltwharf Gulls, and 9 with Marrowfen Owls. The order then walks that club list and wraps, and the earlier club is ahead.

The top two play a best-of-three final. The first club to two wins takes the series. Home alternates. The club that led after the league is home for game 1 and, if needed, game 3, and for every later odd-numbered game. The other finalist is home for the even-numbered games. A tie in a final game is not a win for either side. The series continues with another game until one club has two wins. The final does not change the table. If Lumen Reedmice finish 3rd through 10th, the season ends on the board and no final is played.

The board goal is on screen from the start: reach that final and win the series. Missing the final, or losing the series, misses the goal.

The rules live in `scripts/sport.gd`. Short version:

- Three innings. Four batters a side. Each half-inning is four plate appearances, so both sides always bat.
- The field has Near, Mid, and Far zones, plus one mark. Lineup order is also the fielding assignment: slot 1 Near, slot 2 Mid, slot 3 Far, slot 4 Rover.
- Each player has six stats from 1 to 20: Lash, Eye, Legs, Glove, Arm, and Read. New players are rolled from 9 to 15. The ten clubs are dealt the same stat lines in different orders.
- One tactic per match: Aggressive, Patient, or Field. The final uses it for every game.
- One training focus per week. After the week, every player on every club gains +1 in that club's chosen stat, never above 20. Each final game is one more drill when the final is played. A missed final adds no further drill.
- Each plate appearance is a pure function of the stats, the tactics, the zone assignment, and a seeded generator. The same seed and the same lineup always produce the same at-bats, including every final game. The watch line cites the stats and the roll that decided the out, the mark, or the score.

## Play

Godot 4 is required. From the repository root:

```bash
godot --path lowball
```

Before each week, set the batting order, one tactic, and one training focus. The schedule shows the five matches. **Watch at 1x** shows one plate appearance per second on the field. **Skip to result** jumps to the score. The other four matches are recorded the same week and shown on the result. If Lumen Reedmice reach the final, the same buttons, lineup, tactic, and training play every game of the series. **Restart series** begins again with the next seed.

Mouse works on every button. Keyboard: Tab then Enter, or `1`–`4` to pick a lineup slot, Left/Right to change that batter, `T` tactic, `Y` training, `W` watch, `S` skip, `N` continue, `R` restart from the office or the board.

## Test

From the repository root. The process exits 0 when every test passes and non-zero when one fails.

```bash
godot --headless --path lowball -s res://scripts/tests.gd
```

The suite checks that Marrowfen Owls is on the schedule, that every week has five matches and no bye, that one seed and lineup repeat the same at-bats including the final games, that stats stay inside 1–20, that a scripted series wins the final, that a scripted series reaches a third game, that a tied final game continues the series, that a scripted miss ends with no final, that a watched match logs one explanation per plate appearance, and that generated names are only joins of the syllable list.
